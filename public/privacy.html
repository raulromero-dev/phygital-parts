Your data in Phygital Studio

Updated November 15, 2026

Phygital Studio helps you plan hardware assemblies. You can use examples and save projects in your browser without an account.

Google sign-in and saved projects

Signing in shares your basic Google profile and email with our authentication provider, Supabase. We use this identity to give you access to your own projects. We do not request Gmail, Drive, Contacts or Calendar access.

Projects saved to your account store their parts, placements, connections, notes and up to 40 recent chat messages in Supabase. They are private to your account, with access available to service administrators for operation and support. Browser projects are uploaded only when you choose Save to account. Account projects autosave subsequent edits.

Browser storage

Your browser keeps local projects, chat history, settings, your sign-in session and account-specific draft backups. Clearing site data removes browser copies. Account copies remain in the database. Use Export for a portable project backup; project exports do not contain chat or API keys.

Using codex

AI features use your own OpenAI API key. The key stays in tab memory and is sent over HTTPS through our server to OpenAI for your requests. We do not save it in projects, chat history or the database. Reloading disconnects the key.

Your prompts, submitted images, links and relevant assembly or chat context are sent to OpenAI when you use AI features. Requests specify store:false. OpenAI's account-level data policies still apply. Research can retrieve public product pages, images and CAD sources.

Hosting and analytics

Vercel hosts the app and API functions. Vercel Web Analytics measures page visits. We do not send project contents, chat, images, Google credentials or API keys as analytics events. Service providers may keep operational and security logs under their own policies.

Copies and removal

You can export your project and clear local copies through your browser's site-data controls. For help with access, account data or removal, contact the maintainer through the project repository. Do not post credentials or private project content in public issues.

Shared projects

Publishing a share link makes that version’s parts, models, placement, purchase links and cable details available to anyone with the link. Chat, prompts, research context, private project notes and purchase decisions are excluded. Future edits stay private until you update the shared version. You can turn a link off, but independent remixes and previously downloaded copies remain with their recipients.
